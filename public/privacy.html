RetailPilot

Customer privacy notice

Your retailer uses RetailPilot to record sales and returns. You can make an in-store purchase as a walk-in customer. If staff link a purchase to your profile, it appears in your purchase history.

Your profile

A profile contains your name and any email address or phone number you give the shop. Staff record your agreement to marketing messages separately. Purchase history includes the store, items, dates, amounts and any returns.

Payments and email

If you choose Stripe checkout, you enter payment details on Stripe’s checkout page. RetailPilot records the payment method, amount, status and provider references. If you request an email receipt and the shop has enabled email, RetailPilot sends your address and receipt details to Resend for delivery.

Who has access

Authorized staff in your shop’s workspace can use customer profiles for sales and service. The workspace owner can download a profile and its purchase history or remove a profile.

Request a copy or profile removal

Contact the shop where you made your purchase and ask for the workspace owner. Removing a profile deletes its contact details and marketing preference from the active customer list and unlinks past orders. The shop keeps sales, refund and inventory records. Earlier backups, downloaded files and receipts already sent may still contain older information.

Your retailer can tell you how to reach its privacy contact and how long it keeps records.
