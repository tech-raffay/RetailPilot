Two jackets, one sale and a return.

A customer buys two Canvas Field Jackets at the London branch of Northline Outfitters, our sample clothing shop. Follow the £119.98 sale from the till to the stock count, then put both jackets back on the shelf.

Opens a fresh sample register with owner access so you can complete the return.

Follow the sale

  1. In the London register, search for JKT-001. Select the jacket, add a second one and choose Checkout.
  2. Confirm the cash payment. Keep the order number on the receipt. Exit the register and open Inventory to see the low-stock flag for London.
  3. In Orders, open the receipt and choose Return order. Enter a reason, leave Return items to available stock checked and confirm. Check the London count again.
